APPENDIX 2 — SPECIAL TERMS
Organisation commitments toward participating customers / counties

1. Termination for convenience — Organisation side
The Organisation may terminate the CareFlow service for a participating customer with twelve (12)
months’ written notice. If a specific notice period is not stated elsewhere for the Organisation’s
downstream commitment, the default notice period is twelve (12) months.

2. Termination for convenience — Supplier side (requirement)
The Supplier shall grant the Organisation a termination-for-convenience right with a notice period
that is not longer than twelve (12) months. A Supplier notice period longer than twelve (12) months
creates a liability gap (Organisation remains bound to customers while unable to exit Supplier).

3. Exit assistance
Upon termination or expiry, the Supplier shall provide exit assistance for at least ninety (90) days,
including data export in documented machine-readable formats.

4. Continuity
Critical CareFlow functions shall remain available during exit at the contracted Service Level V2.
