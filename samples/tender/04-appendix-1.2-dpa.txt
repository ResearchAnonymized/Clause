APPENDIX 1.2 — DESCRIPTION OF PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING ACTIVITIES
Controller: Participating wellbeing services county (via 2M-IT coordination)
Processor: Selected Supplier

1.2.1 CareFlow — Personal data processing description

Purpose of processing
Provision of CareFlow clinical pathway software, support, and hosting.

Categories of data subjects
Patients / clients of wellbeing services; healthcare professionals; Organisation administrators.

Categories of personal data
- Identification data (name, unique identifiers)
- Contact data
- Health and care-related data (special category / sensitive)
- Authentication and audit logs

Special categories
Yes — health data under GDPR Art. 9. Processing basis coordinated by the Controller.

Retention
As instructed by the Controller; default operational retention 10 years for care records unless
shorter period required by applicable Finnish healthcare legislation.

Location of processing
EEA only. Transfers outside the EEA require prior written approval and appropriate safeguards
(e.g. SCCs + TIA).

Sub-processors
Listed in the Supplier’s DPA response; changes require Organisation approval.

Security measures
ISO/IEC 27001 (or equivalent), encryption in transit/at rest, access control, logging,
incident notification within 24 hours.

NOTE
CareFlow is listed in this Appendix 1.2. Any Supplier response that omits CareFlow from the
DPA / processing description constitutes a DPA gap (critical).
