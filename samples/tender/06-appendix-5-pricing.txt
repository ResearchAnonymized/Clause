APPENDIX 5 — SERVICE-SPECIFIC PRICING (STRUCTURE)
Customer / Organisation baseline

Systems that must appear in pricing (cannot bill customer if missing):
1) CareFlow — monthly SaaS fee per active professional user + environment fees
2) Identity Bridge — optional monthly connector fee

CareFlow pricing rows (required structure)
- CF-BASE: Platform fee / month / production environment
- CF-USER: Fee per named clinical user / month
- CF-API: Integration / API calls bundle / month
- CF-SUP: Support uplift for Service Level V2 (included or itemised)

Price adjustment (Organisation → customer)
- ICT index adjustment max once per year, cap 3%
- Third-party licence pass-through only if mirrored in customer pricing schedule

NOTE
CareFlow MUST be listed in Appendix 5 pricing. Absence = critical (cannot bill).
