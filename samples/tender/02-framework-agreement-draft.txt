DRAFT FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT
Between 2M-IT Oy (“Organisation”) and the selected Supplier
Service: CareFlow clinical pathway platform
Governing law: Finland

1. Scope
The Supplier shall deliver the CareFlow service as described in Appendix 1.1, process personal
data as set out in Appendix 1.2, and comply with the Special Terms in Appendix 2.

2. Term
Initial term four (4) years from Go-Live, with two optional extensions of twelve (12) months each.

3. Service levels
Service levels for CareFlow shall be at least the level stated in Appendix 1.1 (Service Level V2).
Any lower level offered by the Supplier is a material non-conformity.

4. Subcontractors and sub-processors
The Supplier shall disclose all sub-processors prior to Go-Live and obtain written approval for
material changes. Sub-processors must meet the same security baseline as the Supplier.

5. Information security minimum
The Supplier shall maintain a certified information security management system equivalent to
ISO/IEC 27001, encrypt data in transit and at rest, and notify the Organisation of personal data
breaches without undue delay and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware.

6. Price adjustments
Price adjustments may follow the Finnish ICT index once per calendar year, capped at 3%.
Licence cost increases from third parties may be passed through only if the Organisation can
pass the same increase to the end customer under Appendix 5.

7. Order of precedence
(1) Appendix 2 Special Terms
(2) This Framework Agreement
(3) Appendix 1.1, 1.2, and 5
(4) Supplier offer documents

END OF DOCUMENT
