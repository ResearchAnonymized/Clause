BIDDER TECHNICAL OFFER — CareFlow Oy (sample)
Response to North Shore Wellbeing Area ICT platform tender (training pack)

1. Solution
CareFlow Cloud provides a multi-tenant case-management platform hosted on EU-West. We propose the Professional tier.

2. Availability and support
We commit to 99.5% monthly availability for the production tenant. Priority-1 incidents are acknowledged within 30 minutes. Restore-time objective for Priority-1 is 8 hours. Service credits are 2% of the monthly fee if availability falls below 99.5%, capped at 10% of the monthly fee.

Planned maintenance may occur on weekday evenings with 48 hours' notice.

3. Security
CareFlow maintains ISO 27001 certification for its development organisation. Production encryption in transit uses TLS 1.2. Encryption at rest is enabled for object storage; database encryption at rest is on the 2027 roadmap.

Privileged access uses shared break-glass accounts for on-call engineers. Access reviews are annual.

4. Sub-processors
Hosting: EU hyperscaler (Ireland). Observability: third-party APM (United States region for log aggregation). We will inform the customer when the sub-processor list changes.

5. Exit
On termination we will make an export available for 14 days. Additional exit assistance is available as a paid professional-services engagement.

6. Liability
All liability, including data-protection claims, is capped at 100% of fees paid in the preceding 12 months. Indirect and consequential damages are excluded.

7. Certificates
ISO 27001 certificate attached as a separate file in a live tender; omitted from this sample pack.
