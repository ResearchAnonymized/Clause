ANNEX B — SERVICE LEVELS AND DATA PROTECTION
Sample tender annex for training (not a live procurement)

A. Service levels
A.1 Monthly availability target: 99.9%.
A.2 Priority-1 restore time: 4 hours.
A.3 Priority-2 restore time: 8 hours (business hours).
A.4 Planned maintenance: maximum 4 hours per calendar month, weekend windows only, 14 days' notice.
A.5 Reporting: monthly SLA report by the 5th business day.

B. Personal data (GDPR)
B.1 Roles. The customer is controller; the supplier is processor for the case-management production environment.
B.2 Categories. Special-category health and social-care data of residents in the wellbeing services county.
B.3 Location. Processing SHALL take place in the European Economic Area. Transfers outside the EEA require prior written approval and a valid transfer mechanism.
B.4 Breach notification. The processor SHALL notify the controller of a personal-data breach without undue delay and no later than 24 hours after becoming aware of it.
B.5 Sub-processors. Written 30-day prior notice; customer may object on reasonable GDPR grounds.
B.6 Assistance. The processor SHALL assist with data-subject requests within 5 business days.

C. Information security
C.1 ISO/IEC 27001 certification (or equivalent ISMS) SHALL be maintained for the term.
C.2 Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
C.3 Privileged access: MFA, named accounts, quarterly access review.
C.4 Vulnerability management: critical findings remediated within 14 days.
