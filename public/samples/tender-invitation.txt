INVITATION TO TENDER
Wellbeing Services County ICT platform services
Contracting authority: North Shore Wellbeing Area (sample pack for training — not a live procurement)

1. Subject
The contracting authority invites tenders for the delivery, operation and support of a cloud-hosted case-management platform for social and health services, including integrations to national Kanta services where applicable.

2. Procedure
Open procedure under Finnish public procurement rules (Hankintalaki). Language of the tender: Finnish or English.

3. Mandatory requirements (extract)
3.1 Availability. The production service SHALL achieve 99.9% monthly availability, measured excluding agreed maintenance windows published 14 days in advance.
3.2 Incident response. Priority-1 incidents SHALL be acknowledged within 15 minutes and restored within 4 hours.
3.3 Service credits. Availability below 99.9% SHALL trigger service credits of at least 5% of the monthly fee per 0.1 percentage point shortfall, capped at 25% of the monthly fee.
3.4 Sub-processors. The supplier SHALL maintain a current list of sub-processors and notify the customer in writing at least 30 days before adding a sub-processor that processes personal data.
3.5 Audit. The customer SHALL have the right to audit security and personal-data processing once per year, and after a personal-data breach, on 10 business days' notice.
3.6 Exit. On termination, the supplier SHALL provide exit assistance for 90 days and return or securely destroy customer data within 30 days of the exit period ending.
3.7 Liability. Liability for personal-data breaches SHALL NOT be included in the general liability cap.

4. Award criteria
Quality 60% / price 40%. Quality includes SLA evidence, information security, and GDPR operating model.

5. Tender contents
Bidders must submit: (a) technical offer, (b) price schedule, (c) draft DPA, (d) ISO 27001 certificate or equivalent description.
