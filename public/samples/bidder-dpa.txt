DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT — CareFlow Oy (sample draft)

1. Parties
Customer as controller; CareFlow Oy as processor for the CareFlow Cloud service.

2. Processing
The processor will host case records and related metadata as needed to provide the service. Duration: term of the main agreement plus 14 days.

3. Instructions
The processor processes personal data only on documented instructions, except where Union or Member State law requires otherwise.

4. Security
Technical and organisational measures include access control, logging, and backup. Details are described in the CareFlow security whitepaper (not attached in this sample).

5. Sub-processors
The customer authorises the processor to appoint sub-processors. The processor will publish an updated list on its website. The customer may subscribe to email notices.

6. International transfers
Where a sub-processor is outside the EEA, the processor will rely on the sub-processor's standard contractual clauses.

7. Personal-data breaches
The processor will notify the controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach. Target notification window: 72 hours.

8. Audits
The processor will provide a summary of its latest ISO 27001 audit. On-site customer audits are not included in the standard service.

9. Return and deletion
After the 14-day export window, remaining copies are deleted in accordance with the processor's backup cycle (up to 90 days).
